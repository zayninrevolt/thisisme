A transparent first-pass calculation.

All results use water’s specific heat capacity of 4.186 kJ/kg·K and treat 1 litre of water as approximately 1 kg. The recharge figures assume the stated heat output reaches the water, with no standing, distribution, coil-transfer, control or stratification losses.
